Questions

Do I need a gym or any equipment?

No. Every session is bodyweight only. You need a bit of floor space and a wall for wall sits.

Does it work for snowboarders?

Yes. The sessions train leg strength, knee stability and endurance, which riders need as much as skiers, and the log counts a board day like any other.

Does SnowStreak track my runs with GPS?

No. You log each day yourself in one tap. There is no background location, and the app works fully offline.

My first ski day is less than six weeks away. Is it too late?

No. Start this week. The countdown on the home screen shows how much time you have, and every week of leg work helps on day one.

What does Premium cost?

There is a yearly plan with a 7-day free trial and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices differ by country and are shown in the app before you buy. You can cancel the yearly plan any time in your App Store settings.
