## App facts (the only app claims allowed)

- SnowStreak, iPhone (iOS). Android: coming to Google Play, not live. Free to start, no account, works offline, data stays on the phone, no GPS tracking.
- Pre-season: 6 weeks, 3 sessions a week, rotating A/B/C, 17-22 minutes each, bodyweight only, need floor space and a wall.
- Every session: 2-minute warm-up (march in place 60 s, leg swings 60 s), then 5 exercises as intervals.
  - A Leg strength: squat, reverse lunge, wall sit, glute bridge, calf raise.
  - B Balance & knees: single-leg balance, single-leg deadlift, lateral lunge, skater step, side plank.
  - C Power & endurance: squat jump, skater hops, lateral line hops, mountain climbers, plank.
- Beginner and advanced level, switch any time. Beginners swap squat jump → fast squat, skater hops → skater step.
- Intervals: beginner weeks 1-2 30 s work/30 s rest x3 rounds, 3-4 35/25 x3, 5-6 40/20 x4; advanced 40/20 x3, 45/15 x3, 50/10 x4.
- Weekly streak: 3 sessions in a week keeps it alive. Countdown to the first ski day.
- Season: one-tap ski day log (date, resort, conditions, rating, note), cost per ski day, break-even day vs day tickets, season recap card, CSV export.
- Free: week 1 + first 5 ski days. Premium: weeks 2-6, unlimited ski days, cost per day, break-even, recap, CSV. Yearly with 7-day free trial or lifetime. Never state prices.
